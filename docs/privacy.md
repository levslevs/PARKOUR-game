# Parkour Enemies privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026. Publisher: WaiWai, LLC.

Parkour Enemies is an offline game for iPhone and iPad. The game does not collect or send personal information to WaiWai or to third-party analytics or advertising services. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, chat, location access, or real-money purchases.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores earned coins, unlocked characters, the selected character, the best round reached, and the sound setting on your device. We do not receive these values. They remain available after you close or update the app. The app does not provide cross-device progress synchronization. Your operating system may include app data in backups according to your Apple settings.

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Restoring a device backup may restore previously saved data.

## Support and external links

Opening a support or privacy link opens your browser. GitHub hosts these pages and applies its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). If you choose to submit a support request, the information you include is used to respond to that request. GitHub issues are public; do not include private personal information.

Apple processes App Store and TestFlight services under its own policies. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics you choose to share may be available to the publisher through Apple.

## Children

The game does not request or collect children's personal information. A parent or guardian should handle any support request on a child's behalf without including identifying information about the child.

## Contact and changes

For questions, use the [Parkour Enemies support page](support.md). Updates to this policy will be published here with a new effective date.
