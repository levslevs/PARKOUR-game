# Parkour Enemies support

Published by WaiWai, LLC. Available in English for iPhone and iPad.

## Contact support

[Open a support request](https://github.com/levslevs/PARKOUR-game/issues/new) in this game's official repository. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot can help if it contains no personal information.

Requests are public. Do not include passwords, contact details, information identifying a child, or other private information. Parents and guardians can submit requests on a child's behalf.

## Playing

- On iPhone, hold the device sideways. On iPad, use landscape or portrait.
- Hold the left or right button to move. Press jump with your other hand. Holding jump makes the jump longer.
- Tap pause to take a break, visit the character shop, or return to the menu.
- Collect coins during play to unlock characters. Coins cannot be purchased with money.
- A new run starts at round one. Coins, unlocked characters, and your best round are saved on this device.

## Common questions

**Does the game require internet?** No. All five courses and all character art are included in the app. Support pages require internet.

**Where is the sound?** Enable sound in the game and check your device volume and silent setting.

**Why is the game paused after switching apps?** It pauses automatically so the character does not lose a life while you are away. Tap Keep playing to resume.

**Can I transfer progress to another device?** Version 1.0 does not provide progress synchronization. Removing the app deletes its local save.
